What is Netflix?

Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, documentaries, anime, and more on thousands of internet-connected devices.

You can watch as much as you want, whenever you want, without a single commercial. There is always something new to discover, and new shows and movies are added every week.

Watch on your TV, phone, tablet, computer, or streaming device. You can start, pause, and resume watching whenever you like.

Netflix Supported Devices | Watch Netflix on your TV, phone, or computer

You can watch Netflix on any supported smart TV, phone, tablet, streaming player, or game console that connects to the internet and offers the Netflix app, or at netflix.com using a computer.

TVs and TV streaming devices

Many TVs, TV streaming devices, and media players come with the Netflix app included. To get started, use the links below to learn how to get Netflix on your device.

  • Amazon Fire TV/Stick
  • Apple TV
  • Chromecast
  • Roku
  • Smart TVs from supported manufacturers

Mobile phones and tablets

Netflix is available on supported iPhone, iPad, Android phones, and Android tablets. Install the Netflix app from your device's app store to get started.

Computers

Watch Netflix at netflix.com using the latest version of a supported web browser on your computer.

Game consoles

You can also watch Netflix on supported PlayStation and Xbox consoles by downloading the Netflix app.
